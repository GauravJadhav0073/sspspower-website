Shree Shivsamartha Power Solutions (SSPS) is a renewable energy and electrical solutions business under the Shree Shivsamartha Group.

From understanding energy needs and planning the right system to installation and project support, we focus on delivering solutions that are efficient, dependable and built for long-term value.
